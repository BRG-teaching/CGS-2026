# E. Individual Project

### Objective

The objective of this exercise is to develop an **interactive parametric graphic statics model** to explore and describe the **design space of a structural system of your choice**.

You will define your own structural problem and use Graphic Statics and computation to investigate the relationships between geometry, forces, loads, and support conditions.

The emphasis is on understanding and exploring structural equilibrium, rather than on geometric complexity or computational sophistication.

### Assignment

Follow the methodological steps below to develop your individual project:

1. **Design** — Define a structural scheme and identify the main relationships you want to investigate.
2. **Understand** — Study the structural equilibrium manually through red and blue hand sketches.
3. **Plan** — Develop a flowchart describing the algorithm and the sequence of operations required to construct the model.
4. **Implement** — Build the equilibrium-based parametric model using Grasshopper.
5. **Explore** — Investigate different configurations by varying geometry, loads, support conditions, or other relevant parameters.
6. **Reflect** — Document and communicate your findings.

### Submission

Submit the following:

* **Rhino and Grasshopper files** containing your functional interactive equilibrium-based model.
* **The PDF document**, using the provided template, presenting red and blue hand sketches of your structural scheme, the flowchart of your algorithm, selected variations of the model, and the main observations and conclusions.

### Evaluation

The exercise will be evaluated based on:

* **Graphic statics model and exploration:** Creativity of the structural scheme, correctness and functionality of the equilibrium-based model, and relevance of the structural relationships investigated.
* **Communication and understanding:** Clarity in presenting the structural scheme, explaining the model, and interpreting the results of the investigation.
