# E. Individual Project



### Objective

The objective of this exercise is to develop an **interactive parametric graphic statics model** to investigate a structural system of your choice.

You will define your own structural problem and use Graphic Statics to explore the relationships between geometry, forces, loads, and support conditions. The aim is not to design a complete architectural project, but to investigate a structural idea through an interactive equilibrium-based model.

Your project may be related to a previous design studio project or developed as an independent investigation.

### Assignment

Choose a structural system and formulate a specific question that you would like to investigate using Graphic Statics.

Develop an interactive model in Grasshopper that allows you to manipulate relevant parameters and observe how these changes affect structural equilibrium.

Your investigation should:

1. **Define a structural problem** and identify the main relationships you want to explore.
2. **Develop an equilibrium-based parametric model** using Grasshopper.
3. **Explore different configurations** by changing geometry, loads, support conditions, or other relevant parameters.
4. **Document and explain your findings**, highlighting the relationships observed through the model.

The emphasis is on the understanding and exploration of structural equilibrium, rather than on geometric complexity or computational sophistication.

### Suggested starting points

The following five examples are intended as inspiration. You may choose one, adapt it, combine ideas, or propose an entirely different structural system.

1. **Hanging bridge with branching elements:** Explore branching geometry, support positions, and potentially spatial configurations.
2. **Arcade:** Investigate how changes in arch rise, span, or support conditions influence equilibrium.
3. **Spatial arch–cable composition:** Explore three-dimensional arrangements of tension and compression elements.
4. **Multistorey structure with inclined columns:** Investigate the transfer of forces from inclined columns to an external arch.
5. **Musmeci-inspired bridge:** Explore the relationship between bridge geometry, support conditions, and structural equilibrium.

These examples are **starting points, not predefined solutions**. Each student is expected to develop an individual structural investigation.

### Development and schedule

The exercise will be developed over three sessions.&#x20;

**Session 1 — Concept and structural problem**

Develop your initial idea and study the forces with Graphic Statics before beginning the computational model. Prepare a simple structural sketch, identify the relevant forces and supports, and define the parameters you intend to investigate.

**Session 2 — Model development and exploration**

Develop your interactive Grasshopper model, test different configurations, and discuss your progress with the instructor.

**Session 3 — Refinement and documentation**

Finalize the model, explore the most relevant variations, and document your observations and conclusions.

### Submission

Submit the following:

* **Rhino and Grasshopper files** containing your functional interactive equilibrium-based model.
* **A concise PDF document**, using the provided template, presenting your structural idea, the model, selected variations, and the main observations or conclusions.

Your documentation should prioritize clear diagrams, screenshots, and short explanations rather than extensive written descriptions.

### Evaluation

The exercise will be evaluated based on:

* **Graphic statics model and exploration:** Correctness and functionality of the equilibrium-based model, and the relevance of the structural relationships investigated.
* **Communication and understanding:** Clarity in presenting the structural problem, explaining the model, and interpreting the results of the investigation.
